## What I Learned
I explored two important types of loops in Python:

## Challenge & Debugging
One of my biggest challenges today was understanding why some conditions were not producing the expected result.
I learned that Python evaluates if and elif conditions from top to bottom and stops at the first condition that evaluates to True.
This became especially important during the FizzBuzz challenge. I initially checked whether a number was divisible by 3 or 5 before checking whether it was divisible by both. I learned that the most specific condition needs to be checked first.
