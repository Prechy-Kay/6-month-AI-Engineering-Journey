## Challenge
List indexing and combining range() with indexes were initially confusing.
I had to slow down and understand how the loop variable changes during each iteration and how that number can be used to access a specific item in a list.
Once I understood: orders[number] as "use the current number as the position of the item I want", the concept became much clearer.
